## Rename cost

- Early rename ≈ weekend; before first publish nearly free.
- After publish: new package + optional deprecated shim + CLI forwarder + finite dual window.
- Cost scales with adopters; act before second external adopter.
- Forced renames: tech days, docs/search long tail.
